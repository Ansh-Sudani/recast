Questions

What's "recasting"?
Adjusting a small business's reported profit to show what it earns for one full-time owner: adding back the owner's salary, depreciation, interest and one-time costs. The result (SDE) is what most small businesses are priced on.
Is the draft ready to send to buyers?
No. It's a starting point. Every figure comes from your file, but you should check each addback and edit the writing before it goes out.
What happens to the seller's data?
Nothing is stored. The file is read, the report is built, and it's gone. The written sections are drafted by an AI model from the figures and your notes.
